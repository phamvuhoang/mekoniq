# W08-T02 — CRM, consent and the loyalty ledger

Status: verified_local (software only; SQLite restaurant lab + Postgres-backed cloud relay test). No SMS/e-mail/Zalo sender exists; "consent" records a decision, nothing is sent.

## What was built

- `packages/domain/src/customers/`:
  - `profile.ts` `Customer.Upsert` (personal data only; phone normalised so `0901 234 567` = `+84 901 234 567`; a number/e-mail already on another customer sets `possibleDuplicates` and **never merges**), `Customer.Merge` (explicit, reason required, manager; the source becomes a `merged` tombstone → target, points move by `transfer_out`/`transfer_in` ledger entries, nothing is rewritten), `Customer.Erase` (data-subject request: name/phone/e-mail dropped, consents withdrawn, the name/phone on bookings that carry that customer id masked (the reservation itself stands; waitlist entries have no customer id and cannot be linked), **loyalty ledger and balance kept** under the customer id alone; idempotent).
  - `consent.ts` `Consent.Record` — one record per customer + purpose (`loyalty`/`marketing`) + channel, with a bounded history (who, when, which policy version); withdrawal is a new entry. `hasConsent()` reads only an explicit current grant (never asked / withdrawn / erased = no).
  - `loyalty.ts` the ledger: `Loyalty.Earn` (points are bounded by an **actual captured settlement**: `payment` must exist and be captured, at most the policy rate on its net applied value, once per settlement, one customer per settlement; needs an active customer with programme consent), `Loyalty.Redeem` (venue-scoped balance only; idempotent per source; racing redemptions have one winner via the account's version check; `INSUFFICIENT_POINTS`), `Loyalty.Reverse` (refund must exist and belong to a settlement the customer earned from; proportional to the refund, never more than that settlement earned in total, one refund reverses once; may leave a negative balance if the points were spent — shown, not hidden). Tier is derived from lifetime earned against the policy's tiers.
  - Chain-wide balances (`scope:'chain'`): a venue never spends them offline (`SHARED_BALANCE_OFFLINE_DENIED`) and books offline earnings as `pending` (not spendable).
  - The plan's SQL unique index `loyalty_source(customer, source_event, kind)` is the content-addressed `loyalty-entry` id.
- Permissions: `loyalty.write` (cashier, manager) for upsert/consent/earn/redeem; `customer.manage` (manager) for reverse/merge/erase; `customer.read` (manager) for the cloud view. Feature `loyalty`: enrolling and spending points are *new* work, earning/reversing/merging/erasing finish existing work (a disabled flag never blocks a refund reversal or a data request).
- Cloud: allowlist extended (`Customer.Upsert/Merge/Erase`, `Consent.Record`, `Loyalty.Reverse`), projection kinds `customer`, `loyalty-account`, `consent` readable with `customer.read` only (cashier `PERMISSION_DENIED`, another organisation `SCOPE_DENIED`); page `/admin/customers` (`Customers.tsx`: search, points/tier, duplicate flags, merge/erase requests shown as "sent, awaiting acceptance").
- Tablet: `CustomerLookup.ts` at `/customers` — local search by name/phone, points/pending/tier, duplicate warning, record consent, redeem, add customer; chain balance warning. `LanService.kt` lets the till role read `customer`, `consent`, `loyalty-account` (and `booking`, `deposit`, `waitlist`, `attendance`, `staff-clock` from later tasks). **Exposure to note:** every cashier tablet therefore holds the venue's customer file (phones, e-mails) and bookings offline although the same cashier is `PERMISSION_DENIED` on the cloud view (`customer.read`, manager only); a narrower local projection (search-only, masked phone) is not built.
- Fixtures: `loyalty` enabled; `customer-1` (programme consent given) and one old captured settlement `pay-1` (100 000 VND, dated 2026-01-01 so no test-day report changes) with its closed bill, created lazily on the first `Loyalty.*` command of a lab.

## Limits

- Points redemption records the spend; there is no reward catalogue and no automatic discount at the bill. A cashier applies a reward through a voucher (W08-T01) or a manual discount.
- Confirming `pending` chain-wide points (the authority applying them) belongs to the chain wave (W09-T02); until then they stay pending and visible.
- The cloud customer view reads the cloud projection of the venue's accepted facts; it does not create customers directly and shows requests as pending until the venue accepts them.
- Retention policy is the explicit erase command; no scheduled retention job exists.
