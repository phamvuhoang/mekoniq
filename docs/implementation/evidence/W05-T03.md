# W05-T03 — Split/merge bills, mixed tenders, manual transfer verification, refunds, void and the bill ledger

Status: verified_local (software only; SQLite restaurant lab, fixture coordinator, headless Chrome for the existing e2e). No card terminal, bank, payment provider, device or Gate result is claimed. Card-terminal results are typed observations entered by staff (or, later, a verified webhook); nothing here talks to a terminal or a bank.

## What was built

- `Bill.Split {billId,expectedVersion,mode,parts}` (`billing/split.ts`, permission `billing.write`): `lines` (`parts:[{lineIds}]`), `quantity` (`parts:[{items:[{lineId,quantity}]}]`) and `equal` (`parts: 2..20`). Works only from the bill's frozen line snapshot, never the current menu/pricing, and only on a bill that took no money (`BILL_HAS_PAYMENTS`, `PAYMENT_UNRESOLVED`, `GRANT_UNRECONCILED`).
  - Cancellations are netted first (`netLine`): the line's net amount per component is `netOf` from `pricing/quote.ts` (the same helper the order summary uses, now exported), and each charge group (base+modifiers, discount, service, tax) is re-apportioned to that net amount by `allocateMinor`, so a line's charges and amounts always agree.
  - Every charge of every line is then allocated across the parts with `allocateMinor` (weights = units for lines/quantity; equal mode gives each part a `portion {part,parts}` of every line and hands remainders to the part with the lowest running total, so equal parts stay within a minor unit in the tested case). Per charge, per component and in total the children sum to the original exactly (`SPLIT_NOT_CONSERVED` guard). Parts must cover every net unit exactly once (`INVALID_SPLIT`); a bill whose lines do not add up to its total is refused (`BILL_SNAPSHOT_MISMATCH`).
  - The original stays as history: status `split`, `childBillIds`, `split{mode,parts,by,at}`, lines/summary untouched. Children `<billId>.<n>` are ordinary prepared bills (`parentBillId`, the parent's `orderIds`/session/table/`pricingRevisions`).
- `Bill.Merge {bills:[{billId,expectedVersion}],billId?}` (`billing/merge.ts`, `billing.write`): 2..20 prepared bills that took no money, same currency (`CURRENCY_MISMATCH`). The new prepared bill has the union of `orderIds` and `sessionIds`, all lines (each with `sourceBillId`), summed total, `mergedFrom`; sources become `merged` with `mergedInto`. Merging split children back restores the parent's total/summary exactly.
- `Bill.Close` now closes orders/sessions/tables only when no other *prepared* bill still covers them (a split sibling or a merged bill's sources), and covers every session of a merged bill. Closing a `split`/`merged`/`voided` bill is `BILL_STATE_CONFLICT`.
- Tenders (`billing/tenders.ts`): every non-cash payment is one `payment` aggregate whose id is derived from its external reference `{provider,merchantId,transactionId}` (`externalPaymentId` in `restaurant-context.ts`), so the (scope, kind, id) key makes the reference unique: a repeated observation is idempotent (`duplicate: true`, no event, no money), the same reference with another bill/amount is `PAYMENT_REFERENCE_CONFLICT`, the same transaction id at another merchant is a different payment.
  - `Payment.RecordTerminal {billId,amountMinor,currency,status,reference,feeMinor?}` (`payment.terminal`, cashier + manager): status `captured|pending|unknown|failed`. `pending`/`unknown` set `bill.externalPaymentState` and block every other collection on the bill (cash, another terminal reference, manual transfer, grant issue, split/merge/void, close) until the same reference is observed `captured` or `failed`. A final state cannot change (`PAYMENT_ALREADY_FINAL`). Observation history is kept on the payment.
  - `Payment.VerifyManual {billId,expectedVersion,amountMinor,currency,reference,reason,evidenceRef,claimId?}` (`payment.verify`, manager only): a person attests a bank transfer arrived (`verification:'manual'`, `verifiedBy`, `reason`, `evidenceRef`). An optional guest claim (`Payment.Claim`, still `unverified` until then) is linked and marked `verified`. `Payment.Claim` now returns its `claimId`; a claim alone never changes money.
  - Captured money is applied up to what the bill owes; any excess is kept as `unappliedMinor` on payment and bill (never dropped, refundable). Cash keeps its existing change rule (`changeMinor`), and cash payments now also carry `capturedMinor`, `unappliedMinor:'0'`, `refundedMinor:'0'`, `feeMinor:'0'` (ruling R-T03; legacy rows read `capturedMinor ?? appliedMinor`). Fees are recorded beside the payment, never netted from applied/gross.
- `Refund.Record {paymentId,expectedVersion,amountMinor,reason,method?,shiftId?,evidenceRef?}` (`billing/refund.ts`, `payment.refund`, manager only): `refundableMinor`/`canRefund` from the plan; only the unrefunded captured part (`REFUND_EXCEEDS_CAPTURED`), payment version-checked, so two concurrent refunds with the same version yield exactly one acceptance. Creates a `refund` aggregate linking payment, bill, reason, actor, method, amounts and (for cash) the shift; the payment stays `captured` with `refundedMinor`/`refundIds`. It consumes the payment's unapplied (overpaid) part first, then the applied part (which raises `bill.refundedMinor`, reopening the balance of an open bill; a closed bill stays closed). Cash refunds need an open shift (explicit `shiftId`, else the shift that took the cash if still open, else the actor's) and record `shift.cashRefundedMinor` for W05-T04's expected-cash formula; non-cash refunds need an `evidenceRef`. Refused while an offline grant locks the bill. No order, kitchen, fulfillment or stock change.
- `Bill.Void {billId,expectedVersion,reason}` (`billing/void.ts`, `bill.void`, manager only): only a prepared bill that took no money (a captured payment is never voided — refund it). The bill becomes `voided` (`voidReason`, `voidedBy`, `voidedAt`), its billed orders return to `open` (`billId:null`) and its sessions are unfrozen so the table can be amended and billed again. A split child cannot be voided while a sibling still covers the same orders (`SPLIT_SIBLINGS_OPEN`; merge back first).
- Closed bills are never amended in place: `Bill.Split`, `Bill.Void`, `Bill.Merge`, `Payment.RecordCash` on a closed bill are `BILL_CLOSED`; money corrections go through `Refund.Record`.
- Feature decision for manual transfer: `payment.transfer` keeps readiness `bank` (constant false until a bank integration is certified) and is not used. Manual verification is a human attestation, so it gets its own feature `payment.transfer.manual` (F11, depends on `billing`, permission `payment.verify`, no readiness); likewise `payment.terminal` (F11, `payment.terminal`) for staff-entered terminal results. Both are `new` intent; resolving an already-observed terminal reference is `finish_existing` (allowed while the feature is off, so an unknown charge can always be resolved). `Refund.Record` is `refund` with `finish_existing` (allowed by permission even when refund/sales features are off, capability matrix §4); `Bill.Split/Merge/Void` are `billing` `finish_existing`.
- Permissions (`restaurant.ts`, `rbac.ts`): cashier gains `payment.terminal`; manager gains `payment.terminal`, `payment.verify`, `payment.refund`, `bill.void`; owner `*`. `Bill.Split`/`Bill.Merge` use `billing.write`.
- UI (`apps/tablet-web/src/screens/Checkout.ts`): per prepared bill — cash (partial allowed), card-terminal result entry (amount, provider, MID, transaction, result incl. unknown), manager transfer verification (bank, account, transaction, reason, evidence, optional guest claim), equal split, split by line, void (manager); merge selected unpaid bills; per payment its method/status/captured/change/refunded and a manager refund form. Unknown card charges and overpayments are shown. `LanService.kt` shows `refund` and `payment-observation` to cashier roles.
- Test fixture: `lab.seed('payment', id, overrides)` in `tests/support/restaurant-lab.ts` merges a captured cash payment over defaults and, unless a `billId` is given, writes its parents (a closed bill `bill-<id>` it settled, the cashier's open shift `shift-<id>`). `tests/fixtures/restaurant-v1.json` enables `payment.terminal`, `payment.transfer.manual`, `refund`.

## Limits

- No real card terminal, bank or provider: `Payment.RecordTerminal` records what staff (or a future verified webhook, W09/W10) report; `payment.transfer` bank readiness stays false and is not faked. Manual verification is only as good as the attesting manager's evidence reference.
- Equal-split balancing keeps parts within one minor unit for the tested bills; with many discount/tax charges the spread can be a few units (still exact per charge and in total).
- Split by line/quantity needs unique line ids; a bill merged from split children (duplicate line ids) can only be split equally. The tablet UI offers equal and by-line split; quantity split is API-only for now.
- Equal-split child lines carry the full net quantity plus a `portion`; units sold must be read from orders/kitchen, not summed over child bill lines (W05-T06 reports).
- Money received outside payments (grant reconciliation `unappliedMinor`) cannot be refunded with `Refund.Record` (it needs a payment); it blocks split/merge/void.
- Refunds of applied money on an open bill reopen its balance (`bill.refundedMinor`, existing `dueMinor` rule); on a closed bill the bill stays closed and the refund is a ledger entry.
- Expected-cash/shift close using `cashRefundedMinor` and `changeMinor` is W05-T04. Android: `LanService.kt` visibility set edited, Kotlin tests not run (no device/emulator), web bundle not rebuilt. No new e2e spec (the shared fixture coordinator's later specs assume a single captured payment; full POS e2e is W05-T06).
