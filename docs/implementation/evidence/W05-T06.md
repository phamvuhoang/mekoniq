## What was built

- Pure reports in `packages/domain/src/reporting/`. They are shared unchanged by the coordinator lab, the tablet (from local snapshots, so they work offline) and the cloud route (from the projection). They read ledger facts only and never write, so they can be rebuilt by recomputing.
  - `sales.ts` covers revenue from **closed** bills (by `closedAt`). Split parents (`split`), merged sources (`merged`) and voided bills never close, so nothing counts twice.
    - It reports gross (subtotal), discount, service, exclusive and included tax, and the bill total.
    - Historical receipts and their amendments count on the receipt's **own business date**: the row's `businessDate`, or else its `occurredAt` in the venue zone. They never count on the import date.
    - Payments by method use `occurredAt`: count, captured/applied/unapplied, fee, and tendered/change for cash. Refunds by method use `occurredAt`.
    - `returnsMinor` is the refunded *applied* part. Refunding an overpayment is not a return. `netMinor = netSales(gross, discount, returns)`.
    - Units come from the orders of the closed bills. Each order counts once, on its first closing day, net of cancellations. Equal-split child lines are not used.
    - Pending or unknown external payments and unverified guest claims are listed as unresolved and are never counted as money.
    - Reconciliation: `expected = bill total − returns` and `collected = applied payments − applied refunds + historical cash`, plus their difference.
    - `lateAdjustments` lists facts whose applied/recorded business date is after their effective date (imports, amendments, refunds, payments), with both timestamps. `rows` holds one line per fact with `effectiveAt` and `appliedAt`.
  - `shift.ts` lists shifts opened on the date. It **reuses `cash/ledger.shiftLedger`** for opening, receipts, in/out, refunds, change and expected cash. Closed shifts show the frozen expected/counted/variance values, `recomputedExpectedMinor`, `reconciled`, and post-close adjustments listed separately with their applied time.
  - `robot-kpi.ts` works from the `robot-command` journal by `issuedAt`. It reports the missions those commands belong to (completed, cancelled, needs attention, active), command outcomes (completed, failed/rejected, unknown, in flight) and average command time in integer ms. Results are given per robot along with the robot's `validation` level and a `simulatedOnly` flag.
  - `report.ts`:
    - `buildReport(kind, snapshots, {businessDate, timeZone, watermark})` validates the date and zone.
    - `freshness(watermark)` returns `current`, `stale` (backlog or unknown result), `offline` (isolated device) or `unsynced` (`appliedAt` null). An unsynced report returns every figure as `null`, so no consumer can show 0 for missing sync.
    - `venueTimeZone` reads `venue-layout.timezone` and defaults to UTC.
    - `csvCell`/`reportCsv` write CRLF CSV. Cells starting with `= + - @ TAB CR` get a leading `'` and are quoted; quotes are doubled; values with commas or newlines are quoted; plain integers (including negative minor amounts) stay numeric.
- Lab (plan §4): `Lab.report(kind, businessDate)` runs `buildReport` over the coordinator store's committed snapshots, in the venue zone.
- Cloud: `GET /v1/reports/{sales|shifts|robot-kpi}?venueId=&businessDate=YYYY-MM-DD[&format=csv]`.
  - The route is registered in `server/modules/routes.ts` with permission `report.read`. The registry checks it, then `readReport` (`server/modules/reports/read.ts`) checks it again per venue.
  - It reads only the report-relevant `sync_projection` kinds under `withTenant`.
  - Watermark: `appliedAt` is the newest applied `sync_inbox.received_at`, and `pending` counts entries not yet applied (pending or quarantined).
  - Status codes: unknown kind → 404; bad date → 400; another venue or cashier → 403; no session → 401. CSV is returned as an attachment.
- Tablet: `apps/tablet-web/src/screens/Reports.ts`, route `/reports` (`report.read`), nav entry, VI/EN.
  - Date picker defaults to today in the venue zone, and the venue timezone is shown.
  - A status line (`data-report-status`) shows current, stale, offline or unsynced. Unsynced shows a statement and no figures.
  - Sections show sales, payments, refunds and cash; unresolved payments and claims; the ledger reconciliation (it says a mismatch is never fixed by the report); units; historical imports; late adjustments with effective and recorded times; shifts with post-close adjustments; robot KPI with a simulated label; and a CSV export per report (UTF-8 BOM).
- Dev fixture coordinator (`tests/support/coordinator-server.ts`): the `X-Test-Lab` header selects a separate restaurant lab, at most 16, created on demand. The dev transport forwards the `?lab=` query. This lets `full-pos.spec.ts` run on its own data. The shared default lab and the other specs are unchanged.
- Runbook: `docs/operations/cashier-kitchen.md` (Vietnamese). It covers shift open, POS/QR/kiosk ordering, amendments and kitchen deltas, bill split/merge/void, cash/terminal/manual transfer, unknown card result reconciliation, refunds, unknown print reconciliation, shift close and variance approval, and reading reports including their status lines.

## Limits

- Report semantics:
  - Revenue is recognised at bill close. A payment taken on day D for a bill closed on D+1 shows as a non-zero reconciliation difference on both days rather than being moved between days.
  - Refunds carry no tax/service breakdown, so `returnsMinor` reduces net only, and tax/service are not reallocated.
  - Historical receipts have no line breakdown: gross is their total and they contribute no units.
- Robot KPI depends on commands. Missions still queued (no command yet) have no business time and are not counted. Remote stops are not aggregated.
- Cloud reports are recomputed per request over the venue's projection rows (marked `ponytail:` in `read.ts`). A checkpointed materialized table is the upgrade path once a venue's history makes this slow. The watermark is the inbox receive time of the newest applied entry, not a per-aggregate apply time.
- Tablet freshness uses this device's loaded snapshots: `appliedAt` is the load time, `offline` means the session is isolated, and `stale` means the last result was unknown. A non-coordinator tablet cannot see whether other devices hold unsynced sales; the offline text says so.
- The `.worktrees/robot-lab-runtime` git worktree (branch `feat/robot-lab-runtime`, not part of this task) appeared in the checkout and makes plain `pnpm lint` (`eslint .`) report 777 errors in its `artifacts/` JS. Lint over this repository's own files, excluding `.worktrees/`, is clean. It was not modified.
- Android: no Kotlin change was needed. `LanService.kt` already shows all kinds to roles with `*`/`continuity.resolve`, and every `report.read` holder has one. The report screen has not been run on a device.
