# W05-T01 — Full pricing: modifiers, tax/service, discount allocation

Status: verified_local (software only). No device, provider or Gate result is claimed.

One quote path, `quoteOrder` in `packages/domain/src/pricing/quote.ts`, now prices `Catalog.Quote` (preview), `Order.Place` and `Order.Amend` on the server from the applied menu. The former `catalog/price-book.ts` was folded into it and removed.

## What was built

- Menu data (published with `Catalog.Publish`, stored in `catalog` and the immutable `catalog-version`): per-item `modifierGroups` (`catalog/modifier.ts`: min/max, `size` group whose option price replaces the unit price, add-ons priced per unit, zero price allowed, option `available:false`), per-item `availability.windows` (`catalog/availability.ts`), per-item `taxProfileId`, and a `pricing` profile (`taxProfiles[{id,rateBps,inclusive}]`, `defaultTaxProfileId`, `service{rateBps,taxable}`, `discountOrder before_tax|after_tax`, `discounts` = coupons referenced by id). `pricing` omitted on publish carries over from the previous version. The profile revision is the menu version. No jurisdiction rules are encoded.
- Arithmetic (`pricing/tax.ts`, `pricing/discount.ts`): integer basis points, half-up, BigInt only. Exclusive tax = `base*rate/10000`; inclusive tax is extracted as `gross*rate/(10000+rate)`. Coupons apply in the requested order, each taking its amount from what the previous ones left (percent of the remaining eligible gross, or the fixed amount, capped at what remains) and spreading it over eligible lines with `allocateMinor` (largest remainder), so per-line shares sum exactly and never exceed a line. Duplicate/unknown/inapplicable coupons are rejected.
- Order of operations is explicit and recorded per line in `pricing.steps`: `base, modifier, discount, service, tax` (before_tax) or `base, modifier, service, tax, discount` (after_tax). Service and tax are computed on the discounted amount for before_tax and on the gross for after_tax; service is taxed only if `service.taxable` (rejected at publish together with an inclusive tax profile).
- Snapshot for later splits/refunds (W05-T03): every order line carries `modifiers[]` (names and prices as sold), `charges[]` (immutable positive `minor` with `effect` `add|subtract|included`, kinds `base|modifier|discount|service|tax`, with `ref`/`rateBps`), component totals (`subtotalMinor, discountMinor, serviceMinor, taxMinor, taxIncludedMinor, totalMinor`) and `pricing{profileRevision, policyRevision, steps, discountOrder, tax, service, discountIds}`. Orders and prepared bills carry `summary` (component totals); bills also carry `pricingRevisions` and copy the order lines unchanged, so a later menu publish never recomputes history.
- Availability: sold-out (`available:false`, `ITEM_SOLD_OUT`) and time windows evaluated in the venue timezone (`venue-layout/current`, default UTC) from the command's `occurredAt` (`localTimeAt` in `shared/clock.ts`), never `Date.now()`; windows with `from>to` run across midnight and belong to the day they start (`ITEM_UNAVAILABLE_NOW`).
- Rejections: currency mismatch (`CURRENCY_MISMATCH`), stale cart (`STALE_QUOTE`, also on `Catalog.Quote` when a `menuVersion` is sent), invalid profile/group/window (`INVALID_PRICING_PROFILE`, `INVALID_MODIFIER_GROUP`, `INVALID_AVAILABILITY`, `TAX_PROFILE_NOT_FOUND`), modifier errors (`MODIFIER_SELECTION_INVALID`, `MODIFIER_SOLD_OUT`, `DUPLICATE_MODIFIER`, ...).
- Cloud: `relayCatalogPublication` reuses the domain validators and relays `pricing`, `modifierGroups`, `taxProfileId`, `availability`. `MenuEditor.tsx` edits tax profiles, default tax, service rate/taxable, discount order, coupons, a per-item tax profile and a per-item JSON box for modifier groups and hours. Tablet `Pos.ts` has a modifier chooser, coupon checkboxes, a "Get price" button that calls the real `Catalog.Quote` and shows the server lines/total, and a forced re-confirm after `STALE_QUOTE` (send is disabled until the price is re-fetched).

## Limits

- Coupons are published menu data referenced by id; there is no manual/amount-entered discount and no per-coupon permission or usage limit. Any `order.write` actor can apply a published coupon.
- Tax rounding is per order line (half-up); an order's tax is the sum of its lines' tax. Tax-inclusive service charges are not supported.
- The back-office editor edits modifiers and hours as JSON; there is no structured modifier UI. Cloud e2e was not extended with a pricing publish.
- Availability trusts the command's `occurredAt`; clock-skew policy is the existing envelope handling.
- Split/merge/refund apportionment of the charge lines is W05-T03; this task only guarantees the snapshot is exactly apportionable (tested with `allocateMinor`).
- Android native bundles under `apps/tablet-android/.../assets` were not rebuilt.
