- Ruling 7 (G-CAL1 scope): **`driveModel.axes[k].torque.scale` is registered but not proven by the self-test.** The arm's drive model has an `effort` torque (a limit, no scale), so the plan's "torque.scale of J3 × 0.8" cannot exist on the arm. On a wheel: nominal (scale 1), the 5 rad/s step is traction-limited above a scale of about 0.85 (sum of squares ≈ 10 for every scale 0.85–1: no gradient); on the torque-limited drive (nominal 0.1, hidden 0.08) the speed is a V in the scale only in the first ~60 ms and 1 % apart scales give final speeds 2.5–3.4 rad/s (contact chatter), and a fit over a 60 ms window lands at 0.106 (+32 %). Tolerance not widened; the test was removed, the finding is in the test header and the protocol doc. Cost if wrong: someone trusts a fitted torque scale; any record still has to pass the held-out split, and G-CAL1 does not claim it.
- G-CAL1 self-test result (tolerance 5 % declared in the test before the run): hidden armature of J3 0.13 → **0.13038** (+0.3 %, SE 0.0010), hidden joint damping 1.5 → **1.4919** (−0.5 %, SE 0.048), converged in 3 iterations, objective 20.7 → 3.97, Jacobian condition 7.5, held-out trials (as a measured copy) `pass`; the record is `sourceKind:synthetic`, `toolSelfTest:true`, `produced:[]`. Data: five trials on J2 and J3 (tune J3 step 0.1, J3 trapezoid 0.2, J2 step 0.1; validation J3 step 0.05, J2 step 0.05), position quantized to a 16384-count encoder.
- Signal-design findings (the plan's stop condition says investigate the bench signals, never loosen the tolerance; the tolerance was not changed): the first logs kept the whole 10 s trace and landed at armature −7.6 %, damping +12 %, because the settled part only adds quantization noise to the objective; keeping the transient (−20 ms .. +0.6 s) gave −2.2 % / −0.9 %; the J3 validation signal then lay outside the tune domain (`OUT_OF_DOMAIN`), so validation uses smaller amplitudes than tune. A 0.2 rad step on J3 makes the drive latch a fault (`HEADLESS_BENCH_DRIVE_FAULT`), so tune uses 0.1 and a trapezoid. Written into the protocol doc as how to log.
